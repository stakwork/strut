The root of this person's own knowledge: how things are done in their life
and work, as they have taught it.

Each child Concept of this node (PARENT_OF from here) is one AREA — a
project, a routine, a way they like something done. Its docs say what to
know and do there; its own children are narrower pages, each opened the
same way. Open only the page a request needs.

People, places and other things are not pages here: they are their own
nodes, found by search. A page here says how; it links to the things it is
about.

To teach something new, write a Concept under the page it belongs to (or
under this one, for a new area) with a one-line description and full docs.
Change a page when what it says is no longer true.
