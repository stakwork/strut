description: The conversations between people and their assistant, turn by turn — what to look for in them, and which run a person's words are about.
parent: Dream
---
The material is the newest turns of the conversations between people and
their assistant, oldest first. PERSON is a person; ASSISTANT is the
assistant's reply. A turn with no PERSON line is the assistant reporting
on a job that ended: it is there because what a person says next is often
about that report.

Look for the moments a person corrected the assistant or pushed back
("no", "that's wrong", "too long", "I already told you"), said how they
want something done, or confirmed it got something right. A turn whose
status is `cancelled` was stopped by the person: look at what the
assistant was doing.

Each turn's header names its run, and OPENED lists the Concept pages that
run opened. A remark is about the run whose work it talks about: often the
turn before it, and for a job's work, the job run in the report that
followed it (its JOB line). When OPENED says "not known", that run cannot
be measured: record nothing.
